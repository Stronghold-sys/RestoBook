PANDUAN LENGKAP SETUP DOMAIN restobookid.my.id
===============================================

URUTAN YANG BENAR:
1. Nameserver    → diubah di DOMAINESIA
2. DNS Records   → ditambahkan di CLOUDFLARE (semua, termasuk Resend)

================================================================
TAHAP 1: UBAH NAMESERVER DI DOMAINESIA
================================================================
1. Login ke https://my.domainesia.com
2. Klik "Domain" > pilih restobookid.my.id
3. Klik "Nameservers" atau "Kelola Nameserver"
4. Ganti dengan 2 nameserver dari Cloudflare:
   (scroll ke bawah di halaman Cloudflare untuk melihat nameserver-nya)
   Contoh:
   - emma.ns.cloudflare.com
   - john.ns.cloudflare.com
5. Simpan
6. Kembali ke Cloudflare > klik "Done, check nameservers"
7. Tunggu 5-30 menit sampai domain aktif di Cloudflare

================================================================
TAHAP 2: TAMBAH DNS RECORDS DI CLOUDFLARE (setelah aktif)
================================================================
Buka Cloudflare > pilih restobookid.my.id > DNS > Records

RECORD 1 - Hubungkan domain ke website:
- Type: CNAME
- Name: @
- Content: restobook.pages.dev
- Proxy: Proxied (awan oranye)

RECORD 2 - www subdomain:
- Type: CNAME
- Name: www
- Content: restobook.pages.dev
- Proxy: Proxied (awan oranye)

================================================================
TAHAP 3: DNS RESEND (di CLOUDFLARE juga, setelah aktif)
================================================================
Buka https://resend.com/domains > Add Domain > masukkan restobookid.my.id
Resend akan berikan record, tambahkan di Cloudflare DNS:

RECORD 3 - Resend SPF:
- Type: TXT
- Name: (copy dari Resend)
- Content: (copy dari Resend)

RECORD 4 - Resend DKIM:
- Type: TXT
- Name: (copy dari Resend, biasanya resend._domainkey)
- Content: (copy dari Resend)

Kembali ke Resend > klik Verify

================================================================
TAHAP 4: CUSTOM DOMAIN DI CLOUDFLARE PAGES
================================================================
1. Cloudflare > Workers & Pages > restobook > Custom domains
2. Set up a custom domain > masukkan: restobookid.my.id
3. Activate domain
4. Ulangi untuk: www.restobookid.my.id

SELESAI! Buka https://restobookid.my.id
